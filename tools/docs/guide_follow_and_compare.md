# Guide — Follow Signals and Compare Creatures

Activity: **Observe only.** Pins, ranges, and comparison definitions change
browser investigation state, not the simulation.

## Follow a Drive or Chemical Over Time

1. Select a creature and open its Drives or Chemistry monitoring view.
2. Use the visible pin control on the exact signal. Keep the chemical ID or
   drive ID visible; a friendly label does not replace the identifier.
3. Confirm the recording interval, active/background state, sample age, and
   retained bounds.
4. Read the current observed value and the mechanically Derived delta/trend.
   A flat or rising line does not imply a healthy range.
5. Open Timeline to align threshold crossings or large retained changes with
   observed attention, decisions, scripts, and interventions.
6. Export retained evidence when another person needs the raw samples and
   provenance.

Concentration is a bounded current amount. Dose is a requested signed change.
Keep those terms distinct.

## Compare an Individual with a Cohort

1. Open **Observe → Compare** with the creature selection retained.
2. Choose same-tick individuals, retained ranges, or a cohort distribution.
3. Define explicit world-qualified membership. Do not silently substitute a
   creature that died or disappeared.
4. Choose measures with matching units and evidence semantics.
5. Capture or enable active-view polling. Read each measure's `n`, missing
   count, raw points, median, and IQR.
6. Follow an individual point back to Timeline when its result needs context.
7. Save or export the comparison definition with its world/time provenance.

Interpretation caution: cohort statistics summarize these sampled members.
They are neither biological targets nor proof that an outlying individual is
unhealthy.
