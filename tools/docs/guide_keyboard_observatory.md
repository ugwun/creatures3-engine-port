# Keyboard-only Observatory Tour

Activity: **Observe**, except where a reviewed-change surface is opened for
inspection. Do not confirm or apply a mutation during this tour.

## Orient Without a Mouse

1. Reload the Developer Tools page and press Tab. Activate **Skip to active
   workspace**.
2. Shift+Tab back to the header if needed. Open the world/session summary,
   inspect its labelled facts, press Escape, and confirm focus returns to the
   summary.
3. Focus the workspace menu. Open it, then use Down/Up, Home, and End to
   traverse the grouped routes.
4. Open **Creature Inspector**. Use Left/Right through Drives, Chemistry,
   Organs, Brain, and Genome. On Chemistry, use Left/Right between Monitoring
   and Intervene; leave Intervene without applying anything.

## Inspect Graphical Evidence Textually

1. Open **Timeline**. Tab to a visible event mark. Its accessible name states
   label, evidence, and tick range. Activate it and compare the retained-event
   list and detail panel.
2. Open **Ecology**. Focus a room or agent in the map, move with arrow keys,
   and activate it. Expand **Room data table — textual map alternative** and
   verify the current tick, metaroom, CA value, connections, and agents.
3. Return to Creature Inspector → Brain. Focus the first neuron, move with
   arrow keys, and activate it. Tab to the tract graph and repeat. Read the
   adjacent textual detail.
4. Open **Architecture Graph**, switch to **Text view**, and inspect a node,
   relationship, and metric path. The inspector must preserve units,
   transformations, cadence, evidence, and links.

## Review Change Boundaries

1. Open **Experiments**. Tab through the baseline/treatment controls and open
   any available review dialog. Read the active world and exact effect, then
   cancel with Escape.
2. Open **CAOS Workbench**. Navigate its mode controls and validation output.
   Do not use **Evaluate reviewed source**. Confirm that mutation-capable
   modes expose their additional review requirements.
3. Open **Genetics Experiments** only in a disposable world. Navigate the
   source/copy controls and final review region, then leave without injection.

## Check Help and Conflicts

1. Focus a registered non-editable control and press F1. Read its activity,
   evidence, source, cadence, caution, identifiers, and links. Escape must
   restore focus.
2. Press Shift+? to open **Keyboard and display**. Select compact density and
   reduced motion, then close it. Reopen it to confirm the settings.
3. Disable global shortcuts. Put focus in an input and press F1, Shift+?, and
   Alt+Left; none may leave the field or open global UI. The visible Display
   and Help buttons remain available.
4. Re-enable shortcuts before completing the tour.

## Narrow and Zoomed Repeat

At 720 × 900, or at 200% browser zoom on a desktop window, repeat:

- session summary;
- menu open, arrow navigation, route selection, Escape dismissal;
- creature and chemistry tab navigation;
- timeline event selection;
- ecology textual table;
- experiment review and cancellation;
- Workbench validation without execution;
- documentation and architecture text view.

Horizontal scrolling is acceptable inside a toolbar or data table. The page
itself must not lose warnings, identity, units, evidence, or reviewed-change
context beyond an unreachable horizontal edge.
